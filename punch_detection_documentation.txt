1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Hyper Hitter punch detection system detects, validates, and registers punches
in real-time during workout sessions using a high-performance multi-sensor fusion
architecture.

The engine coordinates three distinct sensory inputs:
  1. Acoustic Onset Detection (Device Microphone — 10ms - 20ms sampling)
  2. Physical Impact Vibration Detection (Device Accelerometer — 50Hz / 20ms)
  3. Computer Vision & Pose Kinematics (Google ML Kit Pose Detection — 30 FPS NV21 stream)

The system is orchestrated by:
  - FreeSessionController (GetxController): Central state machine, sensor lifecycle,
    telemetry event recording, and master safety gates.
  - TriSensorMatrixEvaluator: Domain service deciding punch validity based on
    mounting type, sensor states, and thresholds.
  - TemporalPunchDetector: 5-State Finite State Machine (FSM) tracking 3D pose,
    shoulder torque dynamics, and arm trajectory.
  - AdaptiveAudioBaseline: Rolling ambient noise tracking with instantaneous
    percussive impulse onset verification.
  - DeviceVibrationService: Adaptive DC-blocking high-pass filtered accelerometer
    shockwave detector.
  - FreeSessionPage: Responsive HUD with live decibels, vibration magnitude,
    pose skeleton overlay, hit highlight pulses, and live telemetry log.

2. DETECTION MODES (DetectionMode)
================================================================================
The app supports 5 strongly-typed detection modes (configured via route arguments
or dynamically switched during sessions):

1. Tri-Sensor Fusion Mode (DetectionMode.fusion) [DEFAULT]:
   - Combines Microphone (Audio), Accelerometer (Vibration), and Camera (ML Kit Pose).
   - Audio is the primary triggering event; Vibration and Vision act as confirmation signals.
   - Evaluated through TriSensorMatrixEvaluator with Mount Type awareness.

2. Audio Only Mode (DetectionMode.audio):
   - Uses the microphone only. Camera and vibration sensors are disabled/bypassed.
   - Any percussive audio impulse exceeding the calibrated Target Audio dB increments
     the punch counter directly.

3. Audio + Vibration Dual Mode (DetectionMode.audioVibration):
   - Requires BOTH an audio spike above target dB AND a physical shockwave vibration
     registered on the phone bracket within a coincident time window (<= 120ms).
   - Camera is turned off.

4. Vibration Only Mode (DetectionMode.vibration):
   - Detects purely through physical shockwaves hitting the phone mount (accelerometer).
   - Audio and Camera are ignored. Useful for noisy gyms with loud background music.

5. Camera Only Mode (DetectionMode.camera):
   - Tracks punches purely through computer vision (pose landmark kinematics).
   - Audio and Vibration are ignored. Punch is registered when the Temporal FSM
     reaches PUNCH_CONFIRMED.


================================================================================
3. MOUNTING TYPES & PHYSICAL COUPLING (MountType)
================================================================================
The mounting environment directly changes how physical vibrations are evaluated:

1. Wall Mount (MountType.wall) [Default]:
   - Phone is mounted directly to the wall bracket or heavy bag frame.
   - PHYSICAL VIBRATION IS STRICTLY MANDATORY.
   - Zero or weak vibration (< minVibrationThreshold) causes immediate punch REJECTION.
   - Protects against clapping, shouting, loud nearby bag strikes, or air punching.

2. Pole Mount / Tripod (MountType.pole):
   - Phone is mounted on a standalone pole, floor stand, or tripod separate from bag.
   - Vibration is physically decoupled from the bag, so vibration is BYPASSED/DISABLED.
   - Detection uses Audio + Vision Fusion. Visual-acoustic coincidence confirms hits.

4. DETAILED SENSOR PIPELINES & DETECTION LOGIC
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. AUDIO ACOUSTIC PIPELINE (AdaptiveAudioBaseline & IAudioLevelService)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sampling Rate: Configurable (default: 10ms - 20ms, AppConstants.globalAudioSampleIntervalMs).
Audio Safety Floor: 60.0 dB (AppConstants.minAudioSafetyFloorDb).
Direct Target Range: 66.0 dB to 90.0 dB (minCalibratedTargetPunchDb - maxCalibratedTargetPunchDb).

Step 1: Continuous Leaky Baseline Tracking
  - An adaptive rolling window (default 150 samples) continuously measures ambient room sound.
  - Attack alpha (0.05) and decay alpha (0.008) allow baseline to adapt to gym chatter
    while preventing short explosive sounds from raising the baseline.

Step 2: Percussive Impulse Onset Verification
  - To prevent human voice, shouting, or clapping from counting, the engine measures
    frame-to-frame derivative and multi-frame rise:
      * Instantaneous jump: frameJump >= 6.0 dB (in 10-20ms), OR
      * 4-frame window rise: windowRise >= 10.0 dB (in 40ms), OR
      * Combined rise: windowRise >= 8.0 dB AND frameJump >= 4.0 dB.
  - Gradual sound build-ups (speech, music, breathing) ramp up slowly (1-3 dB) and
    are filtered out immediately.

Step 3: Audio Target dB Threshold Verification
  - Effective Target = customTargetAudioDb (or calibratedBaseDb + minAudioPunchSpikeDb).
  - db >= Target dB AND db >= effectiveMinFloor.
  - If volume is below Target:
      * Flagged as a "CANDIDATE IMPACT" (Sub-target strike).
      * Logged to telemetry for Auto-Config tuning, but REJECTED from punch count.

Step 4: Acoustic Refractory Period
  - 160ms lockout prevents room reverberation and bag rattle echoes from double-counting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. PHYSICAL VIBRATION IMPACT PIPELINE (DeviceVibrationService)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sensor: UserAccelerometerEvent (sensors_plus) sampled at 50Hz (gameInterval, 20ms).
Threshold: iOS default: 0.18 m/s² | Android default: 0.06 m/s².

Step 1: Adaptive DC-Block High-Pass Filter
  - Continuously measures and removes static gravity residuals and mounting tilt offsets:
      dcX = (dcX * 0.95) + (event.x * 0.05)
      acX = event.x - dcX (dynamic acceleration)
  - Stationary device output is 0.00 m/s², eliminating sensor drift.

Step 2: Deadband Filter
  - Values below 0.10 m/s² are clamped to 0.0 to cancel ambient acoustic micro-jitter.

Step 3: Envelope Follower & 600ms Peak Memory
  - Fast attack + envelope decay (0.70 / 0.30) tracks mechanical shockwaves.
  - Peak magnitude is held in a sliding 600ms memory window for multi-sensor sync.

Step 4: Temporal Coincidence Matching (<= 120ms)
  - For clean strike coincidence, the physical vibration peak must align within
    120ms of the audio onset spike:
      |spikeTimeMs - peakVibeTime| <= 120ms
  - Lingering bag sway or post-strike mechanical oscillation (> 120ms) is discarded.

Step 5: Vibration Refractory Lockout
  - 110ms refractory lockout disarms the vibration sensor until shockwaves die down.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. COMPUTER VISION PIPELINE (TemporalPunchDetector & Google ML Kit)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Camera: Front-facing camera, NV21 format, low resolution (for maximum FPS and lowest latency).
Pose Engine: Google ML Kit Pose Detection (stream mode).

Step 1: User-in-Frame & Face/Shoulder Validation
  - Relaxed Mode (AppConstants.enableExtraFaceRestrictions == false) [DEFAULT]:
      * User is valid if AT LEAST ONE landmark (nose, eye, ear, mouth, or shoulder)
        has likelihood >= 0.20.
      * Allows tight framing, close-up punching, and partial head/body visibility.
  - Strict Mode (AppConstants.enableExtraFaceRestrictions == true):
      * Requires both left and right shoulders (likelihood >= 0.35) and facial symmetry.

Step 2: Shoulder Geometry & Anatomical Elbow Inferrer
  - If elbow landmarks are occluded during fast extension, the Anatomical Elbow Inferrer
    synthesizes elbow coordinates from shoulder-to-wrist midpoint with anatomical flare.
  - If shoulders are partially off-camera, virtual shoulders are synthesized relative
    to facial landmarks (eye span and nose position).

Step 3: Kinetic Feature Scoring Engine (100-Point Model)
  - Pose Validity (Max 10 pts): Landmark confidence.
  - Shoulder Movement (Max 65 pts — PRIMARY SIGNAL):
      * Measured as displacement normalized by shoulder width (SW).
      * Evaluated through a Sigmoidal S-Curve:
          k = 85.0 (steepness gain), x0 = 0.018 SW (offset midpoint).
      * Flat at rest (ignores idle breathing/body sway < 0.010 SW).
      * Explosively rises to maximum score for active punch rotational torque (>= 0.025 SW).
  - Temporal Consistency (Max 5 pts): Minimum 4 consecutive tracking frames.
  - Optional Signals: Head velocity, Arm Extension ratio (toggleable).

Step 4: 5-State Finite State Machine (FSM)
  - States: IDLE -> PUNCH_CANDIDATE -> PUNCH_CONFIRMED -> COOLDOWN -> ARM_RETRACTED
  - Transitions:
      * IDLE -> CANDIDATE: Score >= 50.0 AND shoulderMovement >= minShoulderMovementThreshold (0.035 SW).
      * CANDIDATE -> CONFIRMED: Score >= 65.0 AND Restriction Guards pass.
      * CONFIRMED -> COOLDOWN: Alternating Arm Cooldown = 180ms | Same Arm Cooldown = 280ms.
      * COOLDOWN -> ARM_RETRACTED: Requires arm retraction delta >= 0.06 SW, or arm extension <= 0.50 SW.
      * RETRACTED -> IDLE: Ready for the next punch.

Step 5: Face / Head Motion Punch Fallback
  - When boxer throws a close-range punch towards camera where wrists leave frame:
      * Measures normalized face velocity (normFaceVelocity).
      * If normFaceVelocity >= 0.55 SW/s with 320ms cooldown:
        Confirms punch as "Face / Head Motion Confirmed" (75% - 98% confidence).

5. MULTI-SENSOR DECISION MATRIX (TriSensorMatrixEvaluator)
================================================================================
Signals are categorized into discrete states:
  - Audio: Strong (>= strongAudioSpikeDb), Weak (>= weakAudioSpikeDb), None
  - Vibration: Strong (>= strongVibrationMag), Weak (>= weakVibrationMag), None
  - Camera: Confirmed (FSM confirmed or recent visual punch <= 900ms), Rejected, Unavailable

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

----------+-----------+--------------+--------------+------------+--------------------------------------------
Strong    | Strong    | Confirmed    | VALID PUNCH  | 98%        | VALID_HIGH_CONFIDENCE_TRIPLE (Triple Hit)
Strong    | Strong    | Unavailable  | VALID PUNCH  | 92%        | VALID_AUDIO_VIBE_DUAL (Dual Impact)
Strong    | Strong    | Rejected     | VALID PUNCH* | 86%        | VALID_AUDIO_VIBE_DUAL_VISUAL_UNCONFIRMED
Weak      | Strong    | Confirmed    | VALID PUNCH  | 85%        | VALID_VIBE_CAMERA_DUAL (Quiet heavy hit)
Weak      | Strong    | Unavailable  | CANDIDATE    | 60%        | CANDIDATE_VIBE_WEAK_AUDIO (Physical impact)
Strong    | Weak/None | Any          | REJECTED     | 10-25%     | REJECTED_NO_VIBRATION_WALL_MOUNT (Air punch)

6. CURRENT WORKING CONDITIONS & SAFETY GATES
================================================================================

Below is the complete list of conditions that must be satisfied for a punch to be
counted in Free Session:

1. Warmup Safety Gate:
   - Condition: nowMs - sessionStartTimeMs >= warmUpDurationInMiliSeconds (200ms).
   - Purpose: Ignores screen-tap clicks and camera startup vibration from starting the workout.

2. Master Settings Sheet & Tuning Cooldown Gate:
   - Condition: openSettingsViewsCount == 0 AND nowMs >= settingsCooldownUntilMs.
   - Purpose: When user opens the Detection Settings sheet or Auto-Config dialog,
     punch detection is completely paused. Upon closing, a 1000ms cooldown lockout
     is applied so phone handling, button taps, and bracket adjustments do NOT count as punches.

3. Audio Target Gate (Unconditional Master Floor):
   - Condition: peakAudioDb >= customTargetAudioDb AND peakAudioDb >= minPunchAudioDb (60 dB).
   - If audio fails this check:
     * Marked as CANDIDATE / REJECTED (AUDIO_BELOW_TARGET).
     * CANNOT increment punch count under ANY mode.

4. Onset Impulse Rise Gate:
   - Condition: frameJump >= 6.0 dB OR windowRise >= 10.0 dB OR (windowRise >= 8.0 dB && frameJump >= 4.0 dB).
   - Purpose: Rejects background talking, gym music, and shouting.

5. Vibration Threshold Gate (Wall Mount):
   - Condition: effectiveVibeMag >= minVibrationThreshold (iOS: 0.18 m/s², Android: 0.06 m/s²).
   - Purpose: Rejects shadow boxing / air punches and acoustic noises from other bags.

6. Temporal Coincidence Gate:
   - Condition: |spikeTimeMs - peakVibeTime| <= 120ms.
   - Purpose: Rejects slow bag sways from past strikes.

7. Camera Pose Validity Gate:
   - Condition: At least 1 facial or shoulder landmark with likelihood >= 0.20.
   - If camera is covered or boxer walks away, live pose is cleared and vision confidence drops to 0%.

8. Kinetic Shoulder Torque Gate:
   - Condition: shoulderMovement >= minShoulderMovementThreshold (0.035 SW).
   - Purpose: Distinguishes genuine punches from idle body swaying.

9. Refractory Lockout Gates:
   - Audio Refractory: 160ms between consecutive audio spikes.
   - Vibration Refractory: 110ms between consecutive physical impact triggers.
   - Vision Same-Arm Cooldown: 280ms.
   - Vision Alternating-Arm Cooldown: 180ms.

10. Device Hardware Stability Gate (Optional):
    - When enabled (AppConstants.enableDeviceStabilityCheck):
      Linear acceleration must be <= 0.25 m/s² and Gyroscope <= 0.15 rad/s.
      Handheld movement triggers 1500ms hysteresis lockout.

7. PUNCH REGISTRATION & SESSION HUD (free_session_page.dart)
================================================================================
When all gates pass, FreeSessionController._registerPunch() executes:
  1. Increments punchCount (and classifies Left vs Right punch via FSM confirmed arm).
  2. Updates _lastPunchTimestampMs and consumes sensor peaks.
  3. Inserts structured log into recordedSpikesHistory:
       "🥊 [CONFIRMED PUNCH #X] Mode: FUSION | Audio: 78.4 dB | Vibe: Peak 0.32 m/s² | Cam: 92% (RIGHT Arm)"
  4. Triggers UI Hit Pulses:
       - isAudioHitActive (glows green for 450ms)
       - isVisionHitActive (glows cyan for 450ms)
  5. Plays device haptic vibration feedback (if enabled in AppConstants).
  6. Displays live animated counters and updates telemetry HUD.

8. TUNING & AUTO-CONFIGURATION (Session Settings Sheet)
================================================================================
Boxers can tune all detection conditions in real-time without stopping their workout:
  - Audio Target (dB): Slider from 60 dB to 90 dB.
  - Audio Spike Threshold (dB): Slider from -15 dB to +50 dB above room noise.
  - Vibration Threshold (m/s²): Slider from 0.05 to 1.00 m/s².
  - Vibration Gate Toggle: Turn physical vibration check ON or OFF.
  - Vision Confirmation Toggle: Strict (camera required) or Optional.
  - Environment Presets:
      * Home Sensitivity (+6 dB spike, 0.04 m/s² vibration)
      * Balanced Gym (+8 dB spike, 0.08 m/s² vibration)
      * Strict Noisy Gym (+15 dB spike, 0.20 m/s² vibration)
  - Auto-Configure Button: Analyzes recent strike telemetry and calculates
    optimal thresholds automatically.
